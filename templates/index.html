The Problem

Why Most Research Never Becomes a Product

Brilliant ideas get lost between the lab bench and the market.

📄

Hard to Understand

Research papers are dense with jargon. Translating academic writing into actionable business insight is a skill most researchers never learn.

🗺️

No Business Plan

An idea without a structured business plan is just a dream. Most students and researchers have no training in commercialization or startup strategy.

⏱️

Market Research Takes Forever

Identifying target customers, competitors, and revenue models can take weeks of research — time researchers simply don't have.

🧭

No Guidance from Lab to Launch

There's no clear roadmap between "we have results" and "we have a company." Most researchers don't know what the next step even looks like.

The Process

From Research to Startup in 4 Steps

Our AI does in seconds what would take a business consultant weeks.

01
📤

Upload or Enter Your Idea

Drag-and-drop a research paper PDF (up to 10 MB) or simply type your research idea or abstract into the text box.

02
🤖

AI Analysis

Our backend extracts the text and sends it to Google Gemini, which analyzes the technology, problem, novelty, and application fields.

03
💼

Startup Concept Generation

Gemini constructs a complete startup concept: name, solution, target market, unique value, revenue model, and MVP scope — all structured and readable.

04
📊

Structured Report

Download or copy your complete startup opportunity report. Revisit it anytime from your History page.
